Hookin Safety Policy

Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Zero Tolerance Statement: Hookin enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), grooming, or any form of child endangerment.

1. Strict Adult-Only Age Requirement (18+)

Hookin is exclusively intended for adults aged 18 and older. Minors are strictly prohibited from creating accounts, accessing, or using Hookin. We implement age gating during registration, and any account identified or suspected to belong to a minor is immediately terminated with all associated data permanently erased.

2. Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse & Exploitation (CSAE)

Under no circumstances does Hookin permit:

  • Uploading, posting, sharing, or transmitting any form of CSAM or CSAE.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or predatory behavior towards minors.
  • Depicting, promoting, or encouraging any harm or exploitation of children.

3. In-App Reporting & Detection Mechanisms

Hookin empowers our community to actively maintain a safe environment:

  • In-App Report Feature: Every user profile and chat screen features a prominent Report button allowing members to report safety concerns, suspected minors, or inappropriate content directly to our safety review team 24/7.
  • Instant Block: Users can immediately block abusive accounts with one tap to terminate all communication.
  • Swift Action: Reported content is prioritized for urgent human review. Accounts found in violation are banned immediately and reported to relevant authorities.

4. Law Enforcement & Regulatory Cooperation

In accordance with global child protection regulations, Hookin proactively cooperates with law enforcement agencies and reports any detected CSAM/CSAE incidents to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and competent local authorities.
