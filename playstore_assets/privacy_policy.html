Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Welcome to Hookin ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Age Requirement (Strict 18+)

Hookin is exclusively intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. Anyone found to be under 18 will have their account immediately terminated.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Name, email address, password (securely hashed), date of birth / age, gender, and dating intentions.
  • Profile Content: Photos, profile prompts, bio descriptions, and location/city that you choose to provide.
  • App Interactions: Likes, passes, sparks, matches, and chat messages exchanged with matched users.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Anonymous crash reports and performance data to maintain app reliability.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and provide the Hookin matchmaking service.
  • To display your public profile to potential matches based on mutual preferences.
  • To facilitate private, direct messaging between matched users.
  • To enforce safety policies, investigate reports, and prevent harassment or fraud.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell or rent your personal data or photos to third parties for advertising or tracking. All data is transmitted securely using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your entire account, photos, matches, and messages directly in the app via Profile > Safety > Delete account.
  • Web Request: You can also request complete deletion of your data via our online deletion portal: Account Deletion Page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding your privacy, contact our support team at: support@kvstudio.com
