Delete Your Hookin Account & Data

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policy, you have the right to request the permanent deletion of your Hookin account and all associated personal data.

Option 1: In-App Immediate Deletion (Fastest)

Open Hookin > Go to Profile > Select Safety tab > Tap Delete account > Confirm password. Your profile, photos, matches, and chats are instantly and irreversibly deleted.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, submit your deletion request below. Our team will verify and purge all your data within 7 business days.

What data is deleted?

  • Personal profile (Name, age, bio, dating intentions).
  • All uploaded photos and profile images stored in our media servers.
  • Mutual matches, chat messages, and interaction logs.
  • Authentication credentials and push notification tokens.
